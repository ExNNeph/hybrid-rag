# Hybrid and remote work

## Office days

We work hybrid. Our office is in Frankfurt am Main, and Tuesday and Thursday are team days where everyone
who lives within 100 kilometres comes to the office. On the other days you choose between the office and
working from home. Teams can agree on additional office days for workshops.

## Working from abroad

You can work from another EU country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Tell HR at least 2 weeks
in advance so that we can apply for the A1 certificate for social security. Working from countries outside
the EU is not possible for tax and data protection reasons.

## Home office equipment

Every employee gets a one-time home office budget of 500 euros for a desk, a chair or a monitor, plus a
monthly internet allowance of 20 euros paid with the salary. Equipment bought with the budget stays with
you; laptops and phones remain company property.

## Availability

Outside the core hours we do not expect you to answer messages. During core hours, set your status in the
chat tool when you are away for longer than an hour. Meetings with customers always take priority over
internal meetings.

## Working from cafes and public places

Customer data must never be visible to others. In public places use the privacy screen filter, lock your
screen when you leave the laptop and do not take customer calls where others can listen. Use the company
VPN on public Wi-Fi.
