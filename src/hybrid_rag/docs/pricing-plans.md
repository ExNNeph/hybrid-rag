# Talvi Boards: plans and pricing

## Overview of the plans

Talvi Boards comes in four plans: Free, Team, Business and Enterprise. All paid plans are billed per user
(seat). A seat is any person with an editor or admin role; guests with view-only access are free on every
plan and do not count as seats. Prices are in euros and exclude VAT.

## Free plan

The Free plan is meant for trying Talvi Boards with a small group. It allows up to 3 users, 5 active boards
and 1 GB of file storage for the whole workspace. Automations, guest access and the API are not included.
Board history is kept for 7 days.

## Team plan

The Team plan costs 8 euros per user per month with monthly billing, or 80 euros per user per year with
annual billing (two months free). It includes unlimited boards, 10 GB of storage per user, 250 automation
runs per month, guest access and the REST API. Board history is kept for 90 days.

## Business plan

The Business plan costs 15 euros per user per month, or 150 euros per user per year. On top of Team it adds
single sign-on (SAML), 2,000 automation runs per month, 50 GB of storage per user, admin audit logs, a
99.9% uptime commitment and unlimited board history.

## Enterprise plan

Enterprise is priced individually and starts at 50 seats. It adds SCIM user provisioning, a dedicated
customer success manager, 24/7 support for critical incidents, custom data retention rules and the option
to sign our data processing agreement with individual changes. Contact sales@talvi.example to get a quote.

## Free trial

Every new workspace can try the Business plan free of charge for 14 days. No credit card is needed for the
trial. When the trial ends, the workspace falls back to the Free plan unless a paid plan is chosen; boards
over the Free limit become read-only, nothing is deleted.

## Adding and removing seats

Admins can add seats at any time; the extra seats are charged pro rata for the rest of the billing period.
Removed seats are credited on the next invoice with annual billing and simply stop at the end of the month
with monthly billing.

## Discounts for non-profits and education

Registered non-profit organisations, schools and universities get 50% off the Team and Business plans.
Send proof of status to billing@talvi.example. The discount cannot be combined with other promotions.
