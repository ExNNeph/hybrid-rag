# Customer support and service levels

## Support channels and hours

Support is available by email at support@talvi.example and through the in-app chat. Our support hours are
Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 18:00 Central European Time, except German public holidays. Phone support is only
offered to Enterprise customers. Free plan users get help through the community forum.

## Priority levels

Every ticket gets a priority. P1 means Talvi Boards is down or unusable for the whole workspace. P2 means
a major feature is broken without a workaround. P3 covers bugs with a workaround and P4 questions and
feature requests. Customers suggest a priority; our team may change it after checking the impact.

## First response times

First response times by plan: on Team, 8 business hours for P1 and P2 and 2 business days for P3 and P4.
On Business, 1 hour for P1, 4 business hours for P2 and 1 business day for P3 and P4. On Enterprise,
30 minutes for P1 around the clock, 2 hours for P2 and 1 business day for P3 and P4.

## Uptime commitment

Business and Enterprise plans come with a 99.9% monthly uptime commitment for the web app and the API.
Planned maintenance is announced 5 days in advance on status.talvi.example and does not count as
downtime. Maintenance windows are on Sundays between 02:00 and 05:00 CET.

## Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, Business and Enterprise customers can claim a credit of 10% of that
month's fee. Below 99.0% the credit is 25%. Credits must be claimed within 30 days after the end of the
month by email to support. Credits are deducted from the next invoice and are not paid out.

## Escalation

If a P1 or P2 ticket is not resolved within one business day, the customer can ask for an escalation to
the head of support. Enterprise customers can also escalate through their customer success manager.
