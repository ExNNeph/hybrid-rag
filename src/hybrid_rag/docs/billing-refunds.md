# Billing, cancellations and refunds

## Payment methods

We accept credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and SEPA direct debit for all paid plans.
Annual Business and Enterprise plans can also be paid by bank transfer against an invoice, with 30 days
payment terms. PayPal and cryptocurrencies are not accepted.

## Invoices and VAT

Invoices are sent by email on every charge and can be downloaded under Settings > Billing. Business
customers in the EU who enter a valid VAT ID are invoiced without German VAT (reverse charge). Invoices
cannot be changed after they are issued; for a wrong address we issue a credit note and a new invoice.

## Refunds for monthly plans

Monthly plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of the first payment, if the workspace was upgraded
for the first time. Renewals of a monthly plan are not refunded; cancelling stops the next charge, and the
plan stays active until the end of the paid month.

## Refunds for annual plans

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days there is no refund, but you
can cancel the renewal at any time and keep the plan until the end of the paid year. Seats added during the
year are refunded pro rata only within 30 days of adding them.

## How to cancel

Workspace owners cancel under Settings > Billing > Cancel plan. Only the owner can cancel. After the paid
period ends the workspace moves to the Free plan; boards over the Free limit become read-only. To delete
the workspace and all its data instead, see the data deletion section of the security documentation.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, we retry it after 3 and after 7 days and email the billing contact each time. After a
grace period of 14 days without payment, the workspace becomes read-only until the open invoice is paid.
No data is deleted because of a failed payment.

## Downgrading a plan

You can downgrade from Business to Team at the end of a billing period. Features that only exist on
Business (SSO, audit logs) are switched off at that moment, and SSO users have to set a password.
Downgrades in the middle of a period are not credited.
