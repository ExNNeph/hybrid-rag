# Travel and expenses policy

## Booking business travel

All business trips need approval from your team lead before booking. Book trains, flights and hotels
through the company travel portal so that the invoices go directly to accounting. If you book privately
in an exception, keep every receipt.

## Trains and flights

Within Germany we travel by train. Second class is the default; first class is allowed for journeys longer
than 4 hours. Flights are only for trips abroad or when the train takes more than 6 hours, always in economy
class. Taxis are fine for late arrivals after 22:00 or when no public transport is available.

## Hotels

The hotel limit is 150 euros per night in Germany and 200 euros per night in London, Paris and other expensive
cities abroad, breakfast included. More expensive hotels need prior approval from finance, for example
during trade fairs when prices rise.

## Per diem and meals

For travel within Germany we pay the statutory per diem: 14 euros for days with more than 8 hours away from
home and 28 euros for full days away. If a meal is provided, for example breakfast at the hotel, the per
diem is reduced as required by tax law. For trips abroad the official country rates apply.

## Using your own car

Using your own car is the exception and needs approval. We reimburse 0.30 euros per kilometre driven.
Parking fees are reimbursed against a receipt; fines are never reimbursed.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expenses with the expense form EX-2 in the finance portal within 30 days after the trip, with a
photo of every receipt. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary. Expenses submitted later than
3 months after the trip are not reimbursed.

## Company credit cards

Team leads and employees who travel at least once a month can get a company credit card. Private spending
on the card is not allowed. Lost cards must be blocked immediately via the card app and reported to finance.
